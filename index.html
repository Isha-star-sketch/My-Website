// 01. ABOUT

Experimentalist at heart, systems-minded by training.

My core interest is experimental aerodynamics — building the facilities, instrumentation and models needed to understand unsteady flows. I designed and fabricated a low-speed wind tunnel, developed an oscillating-vane gust generator to approximate 1-cosine longitudinal gusts, and combined MATLAB analytical models, Arduino-based control, unsteady Fluent CFD and smoke / high-speed imaging to study the flow it produces.

I also work as an Avionics and Airframe Systems Technical Services Engineer at Airbus India (since Feb 2026, after an internship from Aug 2025). There I analyse technical documentation for its impact on aircraft systems and interfaces, run fault reliability analyses, and maintain system-level traceability across configurations. That experience gives me a systems-level view of how aircraft are operated and maintained, and it complements my research interests.

// 02. RESEARCH & PROJECTS

Experiments first, then simulation, then systems.

Six projects, led by the experimental aerodynamics work.

01EXPERIMENTAL AERODYNAMICS · GUST

Oscillating-Vane Gust Generator (1-Cosine Profile)

Designed and developed an oscillating-vane gust generator producing controlled sinusoidal motion to approximate 1-cosine longitudinal gust profiles. Developed MATLAB analytical models of vane motion and gust generation, implemented Arduino-based actuation and control, and conducted unsteady CFD in ANSYS Fluent to investigate the transient flow field. Performed smoke and high-speed imaging flow visualisation to examine gust development and evaluate generator performance.

Gust GenerationMATLAB ModellingArduino ControlUnsteady CFDHigh-Speed Imaging
02EXPERIMENTAL FACILITY

Low-Speed Wind Tunnel — Design & Fabrication

Designed and fabricated a low-speed wind tunnel as an integrated experimental system. Optimized contraction ratio, test section and flow conditioning for uniform velocity profiles, and validated system performance through measurement and comparative analysis.

Contraction RatioFlow ConditioningFlow UniformityValidation
03CFD · OpenFOAM

CFD-Based Multiphysics Studies

Simulated natural convection in a closed cavity to study gravity and thermal boundary effects, and modelled stirred-tank flow using MRF and the k–ε turbulence model. Conducted mesh sensitivity and parametric studies and validated results against benchmark data. Completed as part of the IIT FOSSEE Summer Fellowship 2025.

OpenFOAMMRFk–εMesh SensitivityBenchmark Validation
04SYSTEMS INTEGRATION · UAV

Point-to-Point Cargo UAV — Preliminary Design & Integration

Led system-level trade studies integrating payload, propulsion, aerodynamics and mission requirements. Performed mission-based performance analysis, stability assessment and subsystem sizing, and evaluated interfaces between structural layout, aerodynamic configuration and manufacturability. Presented as a technical poster.

Trade StudiesPerformanceStability
05DESIGN · MANUFACTURING

Small-Scale Aircraft — Design & Manufacturing

Integrated aerodynamic, structural and control aspects in the design of a small-scale aircraft. Developed detailed CAD models for CNC-based manufacturing and assessed static stability and configuration feasibility.

06SPACE SYSTEMS · CUBESAT

1U CubeSat Systems Study, Structural Design & Assembly

Carried out a system-level study and physical assembly of a 1U CubeSat under ORION Space training, focusing on structural design. Structurally integrated EPS, OBC and communication subsystems within interface and packaging constraints. Demonstrated at Engineering Expo 2024.
